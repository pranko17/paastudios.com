How we make them

Plays offline

No connection needed. Puzzle on a plane, on the metro or somewhere with no signal at all.

No accounts

Open the game and play. No sign-up, no login, no email address to hand over.

Your progress is yours

It lives on your device and is backed up to your own iCloud or Google account — we keep no servers to send it to.
