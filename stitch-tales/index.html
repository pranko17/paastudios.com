Cross-stitch nonogram

Stitch Tales

Every puzzle is a stitch in a bigger picture.

Read the numbers along the rows and columns, fill the right cells — and each solved nonogram is embroidered into a scene in your hoop.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

Free on iPhone, iPad and Android

How to play

  1. The numbers beside each row and column are the runs of filled cells, in order.
  2. Stitch the cells that must be filled; mark the ones that stay empty with a cross.
  3. Careful — a wrong stitch costs one of your embroidered hearts.
  4. Solve the grid, and the figure is embroidered into the scene in your hoop.

What’s inside

Eight scenes to embroider

A meadow, Stonehenge, a picnic, the solar system, a Japanese garden, a dollhouse, a winter village and the sea floor.

Real embroidery stitches

Satin, chain, fishbone and French knots — every figure is sewn in its own thread and texture.

An endless sampler

After your first scene, a sampler of folk patterns opens up — and its patterns never run out.

Hints when you’re stuck

Reveal a single cell or a whole line, and keep stitching.

A gift every day

Come back daily for a reward that grows with your streak.

Easy on the eyes

Soft linen colors, gentle stitching sounds, no account and no internet needed.
