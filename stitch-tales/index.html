Stitch Tales

Cross-stitch nonogram puzzles

Every figure in the hoop is a nonogram. Read the numbers along the rows and columns, stitch the right cells, and the picture comes to life as soft embroidery. Finish every figure to complete the scene and hang it in your collection.
